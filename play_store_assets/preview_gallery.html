🚀 How to Use in Your AppMockUp Template

  1. Open your AppMockUp project where you selected the Samsung Galaxy S21 Ultra template.
  2. For each of the 5 device frames, click Upload / Change Screen and select the corresponding screenshot (e.g. screenshot_1_daily.png for Frame 1, screenshot_2_calendar.png for Frame 2, etc.).
  3. Copy and paste the exact Headline and Subheadline shown above into the banner text fields of your AppMockUp template.
  4. Click Export All in AppMockUp (choose PNG or WebP).
  5. Go to Google Play Console → Store presence → Main store listing → Phone screenshots, upload the 5 framed mockups, and click Save.
